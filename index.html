HOW TO PLAY

  • Move: A/D, arrows, or drag on the arena.
  • Goal: dodge hazards, grab pickups, clear sector objectives.
  • Power-ups: Shield, Slow-Time, Magnet → Phase, Blast, 2× → Overdrive, Revive, Freeze. Each mode unlocks tiers differently.
  • Pause: P or Esc · Fullscreen: F · Sound: M · Start/confirm: Space.
  • Classic: 3 lives per sector, ranks S/A/B/C, progress saved.
  • Endless: 1 life, phases every 30s, drops get stronger the longer you last.
  • Multiplayer: race a bot — Easy / Normal / Hard / Elite / Hell (final boss). Both share the same hazard seed, last alive wins.
